# Humanities Superpowers Manifesto

## Research is not faster text generation

We believe that scholarly work begins before prose and continues after prose.

We believe a fluent answer is not evidence.

We believe an unknown fact should remain unknown until it is verified.

We believe a citation must exist, be represented accurately, and support the claim attached to it.

We believe concepts have histories, boundaries, translations, and consequences.

We believe interpretation requires responsibility, not merely plausibility.

We believe the strongest objection should be encountered before submission.

We believe AI should expose uncertainty rather than conceal it.

We believe quality gates should be able to fail.

We believe a failed gate can be a successful research outcome.

We believe the researcher remains responsible for judgment, evidence, ethics, and authorship.

We believe AI should support scholarly judgment—never replace it.

---

**Humanities Superpowers is not an AI paper writer. It is a scholarly judgment scaffold.**
