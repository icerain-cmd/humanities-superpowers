**Audit date:** 2026-07-19  
**Scope:** bibliographic existence, canonical metadata, and the limited claim for which each source is used.  
**Result:** `PASS` for bibliographic existence and metadata; `CONDITIONAL PASS` for claim-level interpretation because readers should consult the primary sources for fuller context.

This audit applies the repository's own rule that a plausible citation is not a verified citation. Verification here means that the cited record was located at the publisher, proceedings archive, institutional repository, or canonical project repository. It does not mean that the source endorses Humanities Superpowers as a framework.

## Limits

- This audit verifies the cited records and their broad relevance; it is not a substitute for a full literature review.
- Citation verification does not prove every sentence in the white paper. The framework's normative design claims remain the author's argument.
- Future edits to the white paper MUST trigger a new citation audit when they add, remove, or materially repurpose a source.
