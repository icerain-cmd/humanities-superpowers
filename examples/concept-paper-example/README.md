# Worked example: Artificial Nature and Platform Mediation

This example demonstrates the records produced by an end-to-end conceptual paper workflow. It is deliberately modest and incomplete: citations that cannot be verified in the example remain unresolved.

## Files

- `input/research-brief.md`: starting materials
- `output/research-question.md`: question candidates and selection
- `output/scope-boundary.md`: scope and exclusions
- `output/concept-lineage.md`: a bounded concept comparison
- `output/argument-map.md`: staged argument
- `output/terminology-ledger.md`: controlled vocabulary
- `output/citation-audit.md`: verification statuses
- `output/final-check.md`: final gate result

The example does not claim a universal theory of nature or platforms. It shows how the framework turns a broad topic into a bounded, auditable research plan.
