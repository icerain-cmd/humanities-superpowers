# Research brief

## Topic

Artificial nature and platform mediation.

## Initial puzzle

Digital platforms do not merely represent nature; interfaces rank, frame, and operationalize what users can perceive and do. Yet “artificial nature” can easily become a metaphor that explains everything and therefore nothing.

## Candidate corpus

Three public-facing platform interfaces used to encounter environmental data or imagery between 2024 and 2026.

## Constraints

- Conceptual paper with interface analysis.
- Approximately 8,000–9,000 words.
- No claim about all platforms or all experiences of nature.
- Sources must be verified before citation.
