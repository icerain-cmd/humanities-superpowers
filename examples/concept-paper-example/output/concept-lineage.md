## Continuities

The proposed term shares with mediation theory the premise that access is structured rather than immediate.

## Discontinuities

It does not claim that technological mediation abolishes material nature, and it is not interchangeable with every historical use of “second nature.”

## Defensible novelty

At this stage, the defensible claim is not that “artificial nature” has never been used, but that the paper gives it a specific relational and interface-analytic definition for a bounded corpus.

## Unresolved items

All historical priority and lineage claims require verified primary and secondary sources before publication.
