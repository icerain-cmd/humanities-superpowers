# Research-question output

## Candidate 1 — interpretive

How do selected platform interfaces transform environmental phenomena into actionable visual and navigational objects?

## Candidate 2 — explanatory

Through which interface operations do selected platforms mediate the user’s sense of environmental immediacy?

## Candidate 3 — critical-comparative

How do three platform interfaces differ in the way they frame environmental phenomena as observable, navigable, or governable?

## Selected primary question

How do three selected platform interfaces frame environmental phenomena as observable, navigable, or governable, and what does this reveal about platform-mediated “artificial nature”?

## Why the answer is not obvious

The interfaces may intensify environmental attention rather than simply distance users from nature. The concept must therefore distinguish mediation from falsification and avoid treating digital representation as inherently alienating.

## Provisional hypothesis

Artificial nature names a bounded relational effect in which interface operations reorganize environmental perception and action; it does not name a replacement for material nature.
