# Examples

## End-to-end research session

The `examples/end-to-end-research-session/` example demonstrates:

- state diagnosis;
- argument development;
- discovery of overgeneralization;
- rollback to scope;
- citation audit failure;
- preservation of a final `PAUSE` state.

The incomplete ending is intentional. The framework must not call a manuscript submission-ready while a central citation remains unverified.
