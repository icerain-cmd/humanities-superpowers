# Workflow: write a humanities paper

This workflow structures research; it does not authorize source invention or ghostwriting.

1. `formulating-research-question`
   - Gate: question is contestable and answerable.
2. `scoping-argument-boundary`
   - Gate: corpus, period, analytical level, exclusions, and claim strength are explicit.
3. `mapping-concept-lineage` and/or `conducting-literature-dialogue`
   - Gate: novelty is bounded and sources are verified or flagged.
4. `planning-humanities-argument`
   - Gate: every major claim has evidence and a warrant.
5. `performing-close-reading`
   - Gate: interpretations are tied to specific features and rival readings.
6. `structuring-humanities-argument`
   - Gate: researcher material is organized; missing author decisions remain marked.
7. `stress-testing-argument`
   - Gate: serious objections receive rebuttal, concession, narrowing, or redesign.
8. `auditing-citations` and `checking-terminology-consistency`
   - Gate: no unverified citation is silently accepted.
9. `reviewing-manuscript`
   - Gate: major issues are resolved or consciously retained.
10. `verifying-before-submission`
   - Final status: PASS, CONDITIONAL PASS, or FAIL.
